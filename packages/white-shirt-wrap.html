White Shirt & Silk FAQs

Everything you need to know before coming in:

Do I need to bring my own white shirt?

We recommend bringing your favorite crisp white button-down shirt (oversized shirts create wonderful editorial drape). We also keep ironed unisex white studio shirts in standard sizes.

What colors of silk fabrics are available?

We stock flowing silks in Champagne Gold, Deep Emerald Green, Obsidian Black, Pure Ivory, and Royal Crimson. All fabrics are sanitized and steamed before each session.

Can you produce a TikTok / Instagram Reel from this shoot?

Yes! Our Platinum package includes a professionally shot 4K vertical video reel color-graded to a trending audio sound of your choice, ready to post and blow up your socials.

What undergarments are best for silk wrap shoots?

Nude, strapless, seamless undergarments work best to ensure fabric flows uninterrupted around your silhouette.
