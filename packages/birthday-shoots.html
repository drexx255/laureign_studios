Birthday Shoot FAQs

Everything to make your birthday shoot smooth & fun:

Can I bring a birthday cake and balloons?

Yes, absolutely! We love cake cuttings, champagne pops, and balloon bouquets. Let us know in advance on WhatsApp so we set up the cake stand and cleaning towels.

Can my friends join the session?

Yes! Under our Gold and Platinum packages, your best friends or partner can join for fun group and toast portraits at no additional studio charge.

Can we play our own music during the shoot?

Of course! Connect via Bluetooth to our studio sound system and play your favorite playlist to set the party vibe while we shoot.

How early should I book before my birthday?

We recommend booking 5 to 7 days before your birthday so you receive your retouched photos in time to post on your actual special day.
