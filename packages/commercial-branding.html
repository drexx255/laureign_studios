Commercial Photography FAQs

Straightforward answers for business owners and marketing managers:

How do product shoots work? Can we courier products?

Yes! You can courier your products directly to our studio in Nairobi or Kakamega. We steam, clean, style, and shoot according to your brand brief, then securely courier them back to you.

Are commercial usage rights included in the price?

Yes! All our commercial tiers include full perpetual commercial rights for websites, billboards, social media ads, menus, and e-commerce listings without recurring royalties.

Can you shoot on-site at our restaurant, hotel, or warehouse?

Yes! We bring our full mobile lighting and tethered preview monitors directly to your premises anywhere in Kenya.

Do you offer Graphic Design and brand identity packages?

Yes! In addition to photography, our creative studio provides brand logos, menus, packaging stickers, and complete social media templates.
