Graduation Shoot FAQs

Common questions answered clearly upfront:

Do I need to bring my own university gown?

Yes, please bring your university-issued gown, hood, and mortarboard. We also keep generic black graduation gowns and decorative scrolls in studio for graduates whose schools delayed issuance.

Can my parents or friends join the shoot?

Absolutely! Under our Gold and Platinum packages, parents, siblings, partners, and friends can join for group portraits at zero additional studio charge.

How quickly do I receive my photos?

We deliver all unedited proofs on the very same day. Your chosen retouched images are delivered via private cloud link in 24 to 48 hours.

Can you produce framed or mounted prints?

Yes! We offer mounted A4, A3, and canvas prints. The Platinum tier includes a mounted A4 print, and additional sizes are available in our studio add-ons.
