Headshot FAQs

What to prepare before stepping in front of the lens:

What clothes photograph best for executive portraits?

Solid rich colors (navy, charcoal, forest green, burgundy, black) with well-fitted collars and blazers. Avoid small checkerboards or vibrating thin stripes which can cause optical moiré on camera.

I feel awkward posing in front of cameras. Will you guide me?

Every single client receives step-by-step coaching on posture, chin angles, eye contact, and authentic micro-expressions. We guide every millimetre so you look confident and natural.

Can you provide transparent background (PNG) cuts?

Yes! If you need your headshot isolated for marketing flyers, speaker banners, or podcast graphics, let us know and we will provide cut-out PNG files.

Can models, actors, and talent agencies use these headshot packages?

Yes, absolutely! Modeling agencies, commercial casting directors, and talent scouts specifically look for clean, unexaggerated studio headshots that highlight true bone structure, genuine expressions, and flawless skin texture. These packages are 100% agency comp-card and commercial audition ready.

Do you do on-location corporate team headshots?

Yes, we bring our mobile studio strobes and backdrops directly to your corporate office for team and executive board sessions. Contact us on WhatsApp for bulk team quotes.
