Family Shoot FAQs

Helpful tips before bringing the family in:

What should we wear for a cohesive family look?

We recommend coordinating color palettes rather than identical matching clothes (e.g. Earth tones, cream and navy, emerald and beige, or clean white and denim). Avoid overly busy prints so faces remain the star.

How do you handle energetic or restless toddlers?

Our team is experienced and patient with kids. We turn the session into a fun game with games, music, and natural playful moments so smiles are never forced.

Can we shoot outdoors instead of in-studio?

Yes! All our family packages can be done either in our controlled luxury studio or at beautiful outdoor lush garden locations during golden hour.

Can we include our family pet?

Yes, well-behaved pets are welcome! Please inform us when booking on WhatsApp so we can prepare the studio space appropriately.
