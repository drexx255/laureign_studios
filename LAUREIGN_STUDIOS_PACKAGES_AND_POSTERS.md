## 📋 Studio Booking Rules & Deposit Policies

1. **Event & Wedding Bookings (80% Commitment Deposit):**  
   - All events (Weddings, Pre-Weddings, Memorials/Burials, Corporate Summits, Hospitality) require an **80% deposit** upon date reservation.  
   - This guarantees crew availability, backup gear preparation, transportation logistics, and prevents conflicting bookings.
2. **Studio & Portrait Sessions (20%–30% Deposit):**  
   - In-studio and outdoor portrait shoots require a commitment deposit of **KSh 300 to KSh 1,400** (approx. 25% of the package rate) to lock the studio slot on our calendar.
3. **Reservation Lead Time:**  
   - Sessions must be booked at least **3 days in advance**. For emergency or last-minute dates, contact the studio directly on WhatsApp.
4. **Deliverables & Turnaround Times:**  
   - **Studio & Portrait Sessions:** 2 to 3 business days via private high-resolution cloud gallery.  
   - **Wedding Teasers:** Within 48 hours for immediate social sharing; full master gallery in 3 weeks.  
   - **Printed Kept Albums:** 2 to 3 weeks following client photo selection.  
   - **Express Rush Turnaround:** 24–48hr turnaround available as an add-on (+KSh 3,000).
